data projects

interactive tools to explore public data, mostly about Spain, and a couple of R packages to make that data easier to use.

apps

  1. 01

    WikiBarrio↗

    An interactive visualization tool to explore neighborhood-level socio-demographic data from Spain

    open app ↗ in the press El Español· El Economista
  2. 02

    que-estudio.es↗

    An interactive explorer of employment outcomes and salary data for Spanish university graduates across 81 universities and 29 fields of study

    open app ↗
  3. 03

    observatoriorenta.es↗

    Interactive platform for exploring income distribution and inequality data across Spain at multiple geographic levels

    open app ↗ in the press Infobae
  4. 04

    nta-spain.es↗

    Interactive visualization of National Transfer Accounts data for Spain, showing how economic resources flow between generations

    open app ↗
  5. 05

    LiTS IV Visualizer↗

    An interactive online application to explore the data from the latest wave of the EBRD-World Bank Life in Transition Survey, a large-scale household survey covering 37,000+ respondents across 37 countries

    open app ↗
  6. 06

    econphdplacements.com↗

    An interactive platform to explore placement outcomes for PhD graduates in economics across departments worldwide

    open app ↗
  7. 07

    dedonde.es↗

    Interactive online application to explore the international migrant stock in Spain using microdata from administrative municipal registers and the census

    open app ↗ in the press Infobae
  8. 08

    comparatuingreso.es↗

    An interactive online tool that allows users to calculate their relative position in the income distribution of Spain using census tract-level administrative data from income tax returns

    open app ↗ in the press COPE
  9. 09

    datoscrimen.es↗

    An interactive visualization tool to explore the spatial distribution of crime in Spain using data from the Spanish Home Office

    open app ↗ in the press Vozpópuli· La Razón

r packages

  1. 01

    ineAtlas↗

    An R package to provide easy access to granular socio-economic data for Spain at the municipal, district, and census tract levels

    docs ↗code ↗
  2. 02

    subincomeR↗

    An R package providing coordinate-based access to a global dataset of reported sub-national income data covering more than 1,600 sub-national regions across 83 countries

before you write

  • don't be shy! i'm always happy to chat about data, economics, or anything else. if you are a student or just starting out in the field, i'm also happy to provide advice or guidance on the usual stuff (e.g. whether to do a PhD, whether a pre-doc is worth it, alternative career paths, etc.).
  • if you add me on LinkedIn, please drop a brief note explaining why you want to connect. i generally don't accept invites without context (unless we're clearly in the same data/econ/policy bubble).
  • i am not a hiring manager and can't provide specific interview advice for EBRD roles. if you ask me about this, i will refer you to publicly available resources and wish you the best of luck!
